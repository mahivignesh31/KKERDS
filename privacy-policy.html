Last updated: 19 October 2026

KK Educational and Rural Development Society respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website, explore our courses and e-learning resources, or send us an inquiry.

Information you provide

Our inquiry and contact forms ask for information such as your name, email address, phone number, inquiry purpose or subject, and message. Contact submissions also include the date and time. Please provide only information needed to answer your inquiry and avoid including sensitive personal details in your message.

How information is used

Information submitted through our forms is used to respond to questions, discuss course and training opportunities, follow up on requests, and maintain inquiry records.

Service providers and external websites

Our contact form sends submissions to a Google Apps Script service for processing. The website also loads fonts, icons, and software libraries from external providers. When your browser connects to these services, the providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details.

Links to video lessons, social media, and other external resources take you to services with their own privacy policies. Review those policies before providing information to those services.

Cookies and browser settings

Third-party services accessed through this website may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Restricting them may affect the functionality of external services.

Storage and security

Submitting a form sends your information electronically to the services used to handle inquiries. No method of online transmission or storage can guarantee complete security. For questions about where your inquiry is stored or how long it is kept, contact us using the details below.

Your choices and requests

You can choose whether to submit an inquiry. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have submitted, or to ask us to stop contacting you, email us. Include enough details to identify your inquiry, without sending sensitive documents. We may need to verify your identity before handling a request.

Younger learners

If you are a minor, please ask a parent or guardian to help you contact us. Parents or guardians who have concerns about information submitted by a child can contact us to request a review or deletion.

Changes to this policy

Updates will appear on this page with a revised last-updated date.

ABOUT THE SOCIETY

Education as a pathway to stronger communities.

KK Educational and Rural Development Society works to expand access to quality education, vocational training and skill development, with particular attention to opportunities for rural youth and women.

Through KK Computers and its training centres, the society focuses on digital literacy, employability and practical skills for personal and professional growth.
